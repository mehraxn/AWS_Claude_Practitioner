## Overview

Study virtual machines in AWS, including purchasing, access, placement, and operational choices.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain EC2 instances, images, instance types, and pricing options.
- Choose secure connection methods and key-pair usage.
- Use placement groups when workload topology matters.

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Service purpose, purchasing models, and basic connectivity.
- **SAA:** Instance selection, placement, availability, networking, security, and cost.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
