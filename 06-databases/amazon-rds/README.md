## Overview

Study managed relational databases and the availability, scaling, and operational features around them.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain supported relational engines and managed operations.
- Distinguish Multi-AZ deployments from read replicas.
- Evaluate backup, failover, security, and cost choices.

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Managed relational database recognition and benefits.
- **SAA:** Multi-AZ, read replicas, backups, failover, performance, and engine selection.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
