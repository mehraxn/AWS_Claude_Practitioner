## Decision Questions

- Are you estimating future cost or analyzing existing spend?
- Do you need alerts, detailed raw data, allocation, or recommendations?
- Is the audience a builder, finance team, or organization administrator?

## Recommended Method

1. Extract the requirement and constraints.
2. Identify two or three plausible services.
3. Compare them across functionality, availability, security, operations, performance, and cost.
4. State why the rejected options fail the requirement.
