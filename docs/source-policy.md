# Source Policy

Official AWS documentation is the primary authority for service behavior and terminology. Use current official exam guides for certification scope, official FAQs for clarified behavior, and official pricing pages for cost models and time-sensitive pricing details.

Verify date-sensitive claims immediately before publication and annotate them with `Checked: YYYY-MM-DD`. Treat third-party explanations as supplementary only, and reconcile disagreements in favor of current official sources. Never fabricate a title, URL, quote, price, quota, limit, or exam scope.

Use descriptive Markdown citations such as `[Amazon S3 documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/s3/)`. Prefer one useful link over repeated raw URLs. Paraphrase in original language and quote only short passages when necessary; do not reproduce large copyrighted sections.
