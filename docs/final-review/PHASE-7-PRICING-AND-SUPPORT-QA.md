# Phase 7 Pricing and Support QA

## Result

Passed with a freshness limitation. The active guidance teaches pricing dimensions and selection trade-offs without invented prices, discounts, response times, or quotas. ## Areas reviewed

- Pricing models, Savings Plans, Spot, reservations, dedicated options, storage, database, and data-transfer cost drivers.
- Cost Explorer, Budgets, Cost and Usage Reports/Data Exports, allocation tags, and related cost-management choices.
- Support-plan selection, Trusted Advisor, and AWS Health distinctions.
- Free Tier wording and other offers whose eligibility changes over time.

## Findings

- No unsupported exact price or percentage was introduced.
- Older Support-plan names appear only where a dated transition or historical record requires them.
- Free Tier offers were not an authorized Phase 6 implementation target and still require live verification.
- Inline official citations are present in affected lessons; some reviewed lessons do not use a dedicated `## References` heading, which is documented as editorial debt.
