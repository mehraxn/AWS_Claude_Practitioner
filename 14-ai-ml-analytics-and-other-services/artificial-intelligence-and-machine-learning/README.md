## Overview

Recognize managed AI services by the business problem they solve.

## Learning Objectives

- Match language, vision, search, speech, translation, and document services to use cases.
- Differentiate AI service recognition from custom model development.
- Avoid selecting services based only on similar names.

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Business-use-case and service recognition.
- **SAA:** Awareness of managed AI integrations in application architectures.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
