# AI, ML, Analytics, and Other Services

![CPP](https://img.shields.io/badge/CPP-Cloud%20Practitioner-2EA44F?style=for-the-badge&logo=amazonaws&logoColor=white) ![SAA](https://img.shields.io/badge/SAA-Solutions%20Architect-0969DA?style=for-the-badge&logo=amazonaws&logoColor=white)

Build recognition of analytics, managed AI, IoT, customer-engagement, and guidance services that appear outside the core infrastructure categories.

## What You Will Learn

- Match analytics services to ingestion, processing, querying, warehousing, and visualization needs.
- Recognize managed AI services from the business problem they solve.
- Identify selected IoT, business, partner, and guidance services.

## Recommended Study Order

| Order | Topic | What it covers |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [Analytics](analytics/) | Data ingestion, processing, querying, warehousing, and visualization services. |
| 2 | [Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning](artificial-intelligence-and-machine-learning/) | Recognition of managed AI services and the problems they solve. |
| 3 | [Business Applications](business-applications/) | Study notes and decision points for Business Applications. |
| 4 | [Internet of Things](internet-of-things/) | Study notes and decision points for Internet of Things. |

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Service recognition and business-value awareness.
- **SAA:** Selected analytics and integration decisions in architecture scenarios.

## How to Study This Section

1. Read the topics in the suggested order.
2. Write down the problem each service or concept solves.
3. Compare similar options using the decision guides.
4. Revisit the official AWS documentation for volatile features, pricing, quotas, or availability.
