## Overview

Study support plans, support channels, and the role of technical guidance.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain how AWS Support is accessed and what support cases provide.
- Compare support-plan capabilities without relying on stale memorization.
- Relate Support, re:Post, Trusted Advisor, and Health Dashboard.

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** A major exam topic: support resources and plan recognition.
- **SAA:** Operational support awareness for production architectures.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
