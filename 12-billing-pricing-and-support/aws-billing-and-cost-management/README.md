## Overview

Study pricing fundamentals, purchasing models, data-transfer costs, and rightsizing.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain major AWS pricing dimensions and purchasing options.
- Recognize common data-transfer cost boundaries.
- Use rightsizing to improve cost efficiency.

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Core billing, pricing, and cost-management knowledge.
- **SAA:** Cost-aware service and purchasing decisions.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
