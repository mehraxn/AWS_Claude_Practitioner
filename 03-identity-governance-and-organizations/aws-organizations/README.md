# AWS Organizations

![CPP](https://img.shields.io/badge/CPP-Cloud%20Practitioner-2EA44F?style=for-the-badge&logo=amazonaws&logoColor=white) ![SAA](https://img.shields.io/badge/SAA-Solutions%20Architect-0969DA?style=for-the-badge&logo=amazonaws&logoColor=white)

## Overview

Learn how multiple AWS accounts are grouped, governed, and restricted centrally.

## Learning Objectives

- Explain organizations, organizational units, and management/member accounts.
- Understand how service control policies limit maximum permissions.
- Relate Organizations to multi-account governance.

## Lesson Order

| Order | Topic | What it covers |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [AWS Organizations](01-overview.md) | Study notes and decision points for AWS Organizations. |
| 2 | [Service Control Policy (SCP)](02-service-control-policies.md) | Study notes and decision points for Service Control Policy (SCP). |

## Certification Focus

- **CPP:** Recognize centralized account governance and consolidated management.
- **SAA:** Use account boundaries and SCPs in multi-account designs.

## Study Tips

- Focus on the service purpose before memorizing features.
- Note the requirements that make this service a better or worse choice than alternatives.
- Review security, availability, operations, and cost together.
